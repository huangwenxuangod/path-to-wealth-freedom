# Tutorial Overlay Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
Warm editorial tutorial graphics over real vertical talking-head footage. The overlay behaves like a confident creator toolkit: mustard labels, ink typography, paper cards, hand-drawn circles, and precise progress cues. It avoids the speaker's face and keeps the footage dominant.

## Colors
- Ink: `#17130F`
- Paper: `#FFF8E8`
- Accent: `#F6C945`
- Signal: `#E95D3C`
- Soft shadow: `rgba(23,19,15,.22)`

## Typography
- Display: Arial Black / Arial heavyweight
- Supporting: Georgia editorial serif

## Motion
Fast masked rises, springy card landings, underline draws, and restrained holds. Every overlay exits before the next focal card takes over.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not cover the speaker's eyes or mouth
- No purple-blue gradients
- No glassmorphism
- No tiny web-UI text
- No infinite animation
