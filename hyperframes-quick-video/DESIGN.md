# Astra / Signal Noir

## Style Prompt
一种偏纪录片式的未来科技视觉：黑色铅灰底、酸橙绿信号色、锐利的排版和数据面板。画面要像一台正在思考的高性能系统，信息密度高但留白明确，拒绝廉价霓虹和模板化卡片。

## Colors
- `#101317` background / graphite black
- `#E7FF4F` primary accent / signal lime
- `#F4F1E8` primary text / warm white
- `#9AA3A8` secondary text / cool gray
- `#24323A` panel / blue graphite

## Typography
- Headlines: `Arial Narrow`, `PingFang SC`, and `Songti SC`, 700-900
- Closing statement: `Kaiti SC`; body and data: `Menlo`, monospace, 400

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use purple-blue gradients or generic cyan neon.
- Do not use rounded app-card grids.
- Do not claim unsupported benchmark scores.
- Do not use tiny web-sized text.
