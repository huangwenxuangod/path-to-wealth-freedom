## HOW I DID IT...

If you don't know by now, AI girls are one of the fastest ways to make money online in 2026. I've been running my own AI girls for 2+ years & made over $150k in the process. During that time, I've tested countless different strategies, tools, & workflows, but this is one of the simplest and most automated systems I've built so far.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLaSVhKWAAAwtqo?format=jpg&name=large)

One thing I've always wished, though, is if I could completely take my hands off the work, if I could build a system that was truly automated, that actually runs by itself, that still works. Such things have always been promised with AI, but could I truly make this vision possible?

**Well, here's exactly how I did just that.**

# STEP ONE: CREATE YOUR CHARACTER

The first step to any good AI girl is going to be picking a name, picking a niche, & picking a visual identity. So, in order for this to be as easy & fast as possible, I just needed to use Claude to help decide what each of these 3 things were going to be for this new project.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLaSbOWX0AEUThu?format=jpg&name=large)

I used the new Claude Fable 5 & asked it to deeply research & consider what were the hottest trending niches within the AI girl space, ones that weren't oversaturated but growing quickly. I then asked it to tell me what visual identity options I would have for somebody within this niche.

The answer was clear: I was going to build an emo/alt e-girl account. The visual identity it suggested was pretty similar for all 3 suggestions, so we settled on a white girl aged about 20 with black hair, a fringe, heavy eyeliner, & emo/gothic makeup.

# STEP TWO: SETTING EVERYTHING UP

The next step was going to be laying the foundations for the social media I would require & setting up our actual Fanvue account so I could eventually make money. For this, I just needed some bio template options & a profile picture, which I would also use as my main face swap reference photo.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLaSg3xXMAAXCou?format=jpg&name=large)

I went over to ComfyUI, where I already have my pre-built workflows. These include a Flux-based workflow for simple content creation & a Flux-based workflow with a not-safe-for-work LoRA, which is able to create the not-safe-for-work content for the actual Fanvue content we would sell.

I set my aspect ratio to a square, asked Claude for the exact face prompt I would need to create this girl, made the image & posted it as the profile picture on both accounts.

# STEP THREE: LET'S CREATE CONTENT

Now I was going to need the rest of my Instagram photo grid content, my Fanvue wall content, & the first few Instagram reels I would create in a hope to go viral.

The key here was speed. I trusted the reliability of my Flux workflow in ComfyUI & knew Claude could generate similar prompts that would produce the results I needed for each content type. I set my aspect ratio to 4:5 & asked Claude for 6 prompts to build out my first 6 Instagram posts.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLaSm2ZXkAA4aqQ?format=jpg&name=large)

I scheduled these across the coming week. For the Fanvue wall content, I wanted a few posts ready before anyone subscribed, so I asked Claude for 5 semi-NSFW content prompts. I wanted enough to create curiosity, but not the full reveal people would later pay for.

I used the same process to build a bank of NSFW photo content. I still don't think NSFW video is quite good enough to tell a realistic story, especially if you're aiming for maximum speed & automation.

# STEP FOUR: INSTAGRAM REELS BREAKDOWN

Your go-to AI image-to-video software should be Kling 3.0. You'll need a 9:16 image of your AI creator, then ask Claude for ideas on what to create. You only need 4 to 5 concepts, whether that's swaying side to side, leaning towards the camera, walking into frame, or dancing seductively. I asked Claude for prompts covering each motion type & saved the best ones.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLaStxMWEAAPWqL?format=jpg&name=large)

Next, I asked Claude, using Fable 5, to research the types of on-screen text that gave AI girl content the best chance of going viral. This is the key part. The motion can be simple. The main thing is the text on screen & the story you're telling.

I asked Claude for 30 ideas & produced over 50 reels in a single day using CapCut. I scheduled 3 reels per day on Instagram & focused on whatever performed best.

It took around 5 days to get my first subscriber. After one reel hit 10,000 views, another went viral, reaching over 600,000 views. The page grew to over 800 followers & 50 Fanvue subscribers, allowing me to begin using the new Fanvue MCP.

# STEP FIVE: WHAT IS THE FANVUE MCP?

I had one advantage to all my other competitors: early access to Fanvue's new release, the MCP. Fanvue MCP is a brand new tool which allows you to connect your Fanvue account to Claude & analyse your conversations with customers, see what types of content are performing well on your profile, what pricing you should set, & more.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HLaS2WcWUAA92ui?format=jpg&name=large)

I asked Claude to do a full breakdown using the Fanvue MCP & tell me what posts I should create, what prices I should set, & how I should reply to customers. I set it up to notify me whenever someone responded & generate a draft reply using my existing messaging style.

I now had a clear workflow. Each day, I would log on to Claude & use the Fanvue MCP to reply to fans, analyse my Instagram profile, identify what content was working, & generate new content ideas.

I could spend around 40 minutes a day doing this, scheduling posts, & run a system inside a work lunch break that had the potential to generate serious results.

# STEP SIX: THE RESULTS

In my first month, I generated $600 in revenue, but in the second month, one of my reels went viral, hitting 600,000 views, & I was able to generate over $5,300 in a single month.

This was with minimal spend on my part, heavy automation from Claude & the FanVue MCP, & all within a 40-minute work window each & every day.
