# Quick Video Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
A high-energy kinetic-type demo that moves through graphic design references with bold editorial pacing, sharp wipes, and decisive typographic hierarchy.

## Colors
- Canvas: `#0A0A0F`
- Foreground: `#F7F4ED`
- Accent red: `#FF3B30`
- Accent yellow: `#FFD60A`
- Cool support: `#3A86FF`

## Typography
- Display: heavyweight grotesque sans for kinetic statements
- Editorial contrast: high-contrast serif for brief emphasis

## Motion
Fast 0.3-0.7s entrances, crisp wipes, overshoot used sparingly, no infinite loops.

## What NOT to Do
- No generic purple-blue gradients
- No decorative card grids
- No low-contrast gray text
- No slow, floaty entrances
- No random or time-dependent animation
