## 2. Mindset Setup (3 min must-read)

### 2.1 The 4 Truths of Affiliate Marketing

| Truth | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Truth 1: 80/20 Rule** | 80% of your income comes from 20% of your programs. **Don't spread thin — focus on 3-5 first** |
| **Truth 2: No single partner >50%** | Don't put all eggs in one basket. Amazon cut home decor commission 8%→3% with just 1 week notice in 2020 |
| **Truth 3: 30-60 day payout cycle** | You refer a sale, but you get paid 30-60 days later. **You need some cash runway upfront** |
| **Truth 4: Disclosure required** | US FTC, China Internet Ad Rules require you to label "this is an ad/affiliate link." **No disclosure = lost trust + fines** |

### 2.2 The 3 Selection Principles (Core Doctrine)

1. **High Rate + Long Cookie**: 30%+ commission + 90+ day cookie wins
2. **High Ticket Size**: $50+/mo products give bigger payouts per sale
3. **High Renewal Rate**: SaaS subscription > one-time products

## 5. Pitfalls Guide (15 Traps, Must Read)

⚠️ **Legal/Compliance Traps**
1. **No disclosure → lost trust + fines**: Always state "this post/video contains affiliate links"
2. **Domain with brand name → rejected**: Don't use `jasper-affiliate.com`
3. **Exaggerating effects → complaints**: Reviews must list pros AND cons
4. **Fake income claims → legal risk**: Don't promise "earn $10k/mo with X"

⚠️ **Revenue Traps**
5. **Over-dependence on single partner**: No single program > 50% of income
6. **Don't read T&Cs → commission cut**: Amazon/Shopify both changed rules
7. **High payout threshold**: Pick $50-$100 minimum, not $500
8. **Cookie too short**: 1-day/Session = wasted effort, **30-day is industry baseline**
9. **Merchant delays payment / scams**: Choose big brands, read T&Cs first
10. **Program suddenly ends**: Airbnb, Hootsuite both shut down affiliate programs

⚠️ **Operational Traps**
11. **No disclosure**: One report = -points, three = banned
12. **Only chase trends, no moat**: Algorithm changes = you die. Build email list
13. **Don't review data**: Must review clicks/conversions/revenue monthly
14. **Repeatedly apply to rejected programs**: 2 rejections = move on
15. **A/B test not continuous**: Colors, copy, CTAs, titles — all need to rotate
