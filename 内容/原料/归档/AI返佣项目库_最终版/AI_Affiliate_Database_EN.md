1. **Sell Shovels** — AI relay stations / tool distribution (low barrier)

3. **Service Provider** — AI contract work / consulting / operations (high ticket)
4. **Product Builder** — Wrapped apps / Agents / vertical SaaS (high margin)
5. **Education** — AI courses / bootcamps / knowledge communities (passive income)
6. **IP Building** — Digital humans / virtual streamers / AI model accounts (traffic)
7. **E-commerce** — AI product images / selection / listing optimization (commission)
8. **Investment** — AI stocks / crypto / project dividends (high risk high return)
