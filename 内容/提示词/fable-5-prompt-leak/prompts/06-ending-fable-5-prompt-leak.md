# Prompt 06 · 结尾启示

## 内容定位
- **核心金句**：「潘多拉的盒子被打开之后，是没办法再关上的」
- **核心洞察**：提示词工程的地位从"调参小技巧" → "AI 产品核心竞争力"
- **未来判断**：未来 3 年 AI 公司之间的竞争是"谁的 prompt 更精细、更可审计、更可继承"
- **氛围**：极简、金句、报刊感、红色印章

## 完整 Prompt

```text
A bold screen-print style editorial closing card, portrait 3:4 aspect ratio, dramatic duotone limited color palette of crimson red (#DC143C) and deep navy black (#0D1B2A) on warm cream paper background (#F5E6D0).

The composition is sparse and powerful. Centered in the upper third, a large bold condensed sans-serif quote in crimson: "潘多拉的盒子打开了" followed by a smaller line in navy: "就再也没办法关上"

Below that, a small decorative crimson divider line with star-burst elements at both ends.

In the lower third, three short lines of bold navy condensed sans-serif stacked vertically:
- "提示词工程 = AI 产品核心竞争力"
- "未来 3 年 = 谁更精细 · 更可审计"
- "Fable 5 灵魂已被永久开源"

Bottom right corner features a vintage crimson stamp-like circular seal with Chinese characters "已开源" inside, slightly rotated, looking like a document stamp.

Vintage editorial border with thin crimson frame. Halftone dot patterns subtly visible. Maximum 3 colors. The composition breathes with negative space. Sparse, powerful, memorable.
```

## 注意事项
- 极简，必须有大量留白
- 红色印章是亮点，必须像真的印章
- 三行洞察要垂直对齐
- 整体节奏舒缓，给读者留下回味空间
