# 留白 / The Shape of Silence
## Style Prompt
A 32-second moving print in four movements: eclipse, wind-carved strata, nocturnal water, and a vermilion seal. Copperplate linework meets Japanese negative space and cinematic light. Every frame is a landscape, not a slide. Materials and one continuous circular motif carry the meaning.
## Colors
- #111b20 ink, ocean shadows
- #efe5cf uncoated paper and light
- #b88a54 brushed brass and sand
- #a54232 vermilion seal
- #527b7c patinated water
## Typography
Songti SC for the title and poetic fragments; Helvetica Neue light for tiny editorial captions. Titles 96px, prose 28px. Sparse, deliberate placements outside the image focal point.
## Motion
Slow parallax, concentric breathing, matched circles. Incoming scene dissolves over fully present outgoing image over 1.6s. Finite deterministic motion. 24fps film cadence.
## What NOT to Do
No capability claims, UI cards, neon, flashy cuts, progress bars, unmotivated text, stock footage or template music.
